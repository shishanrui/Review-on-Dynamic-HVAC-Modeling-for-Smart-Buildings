# Curated Paper Lists for Dynamic HVAC Modeling in Smart Buildings

This repository accompanies the review paper:

> **Review on dynamic HVAC modeling for smart buildings: advancing fault detection and diagnosis, control optimization, and demand-side energy flexibility**

It provides the curated and structured paper lists used to support the systematic analysis and synthesis presented in the review.

The repository covers peer-reviewed studies on **dynamic modeling of HVAC systems in smart buildings**, with particular attention to how dynamic models are constructed, calibrated, validated, and applied in different operational contexts.

## Paper groups

The repository contains two complementary paper groups corresponding to different roles of dynamic models in the literature.

### Group 1: Dynamic models applied to operational tasks

This group includes **181 studies** that apply dynamic HVAC models to one or more of the following operational domains:

- **Control optimization (CO)**
- **Demand-side energy flexibility (EF)**
- **Fault detection and diagnosis (FDD)**

These studies form the core evidence base discussed in the main body of the review. They are analyzed in terms of:

- Modeling paradigm and complexity
- Calibration and validation practices
- Control or diagnostic time scales
- Evaluation settings, including simulation, laboratory experiments, hardware-in-the-loop (HIL) testing, and real buildings
- Deployment maturity and reported performance outcomes

### Group 2: Dynamic model development and related applications

This group includes **184 studies** that focus primarily on:

- Novel dynamic model formulations or identification methods
- Model structure development and validation
- Related applications, such as system sizing, design, and offline analysis

These studies provide methodological foundations and contextual background but are not the primary focus of the operational synthesis in the review.
